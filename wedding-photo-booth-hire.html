Wedding Entertainment

The Best Wedding Entertainment in Ireland

A photo booth is consistently rated as one of the top wedding entertainment ideas in Ireland. While the band takes a break, while guests wait for the evening buffet, or as the night winds down, the photo booth keeps everyone entertained.

Unlike a wedding band or DJ that only some guests enjoy, a photo booth brings every generation together — from grandparents to flower girls, everyone queues up for their turn. It is the one piece of wedding entertainment that creates physical keepsakes your guests take home and keep.

  • Keeps guests entertained between courses & band breaks
  • Brings all ages together — kids to grandparents
  • Custom prints double as a wedding favour
  • Guest photo album included with every wedding booking
