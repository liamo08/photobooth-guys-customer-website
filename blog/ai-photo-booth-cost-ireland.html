How Much Does an AI Photo Booth Cost in Ireland? 2026 Pricing Guide

The AI photo booth is the fastest-growing request on our books, and the question that lands with almost every enquiry is the same one. How much does an AI photo booth actually cost in Ireland?

Short answer: AI photo booth hire in Ireland starts from €900 for a standard, fully inclusive 3-hour package. That puts it about €220 above a basic open-air booth, and the gap is real — it pays for live AI image processing on every shot, the in-house software that runs it, higher-spec capture, and a bigger creative setup. This guide breaks down exactly what you are paying for and how to get the most from your budget.

AI Photo Booth Price at a Glance

Here is what AI photo booth hire costs in Ireland in 2026:

  • Standard AI booth (3 hours): from €900, fully inclusive
  • AI booth + extra hour: typically €100–€120 per additional hour
  • AI + open-air bundle: from around €1,400 — gives a print option for the older crowd while the AI booth handles the social-first guests, and saves against hiring both separately
  • AI + 360 bundle: from around €1,500 — the two most-shared formats in one booking, at a lower combined rate
  • Corporate branded AI activation: sits above the base — typically a few hundred euro more depending on the scope of custom AI styles, branded scenes and data capture

What Is Included in AI Photo Booth Hire?

Every AI photo booth hire we deliver across Ireland comes with the same fully inclusive package. The €900 is the number — there are no surprise add-ons waiting on the day:

  • Delivery, setup and pack-down — we arrive well before guests do, build the setup, dial in the lighting and run the AI styles through a test pass; we pack down quietly after the event
  • Professional booth attendant — runs the booth for the full 3 hours, briefs every guest, and guides people through picking a style, which matters more with AI than with a point-and-grin booth
  • Unlimited prints — high-quality dye-sublimation prints on the night, with no cap on the number guests take home
  • Instant digital sharing — every styled image lands on the guest’s phone within seconds by SMS or email, ready to post straight away
  • Custom photo overlay — names, dates, hashtags or branding worked into the print and the digital file
  • Online event gallery — every image from the night in one shareable link the morning after
  • A curated set of AI styles — from painterly portraits to themed scenes, chosen to suit your event rather than a random grab-bag
  • Props and studio lighting — the same polished finish we bring to every booth

All of it runs on BoothLedger, the photo-booth software we build and maintain in-house. The same team that engineers the AI pipeline is the team running your event, so when something needs a tweak at 9pm on a Saturday, we are not waiting on a vendor in another timezone — we fix it on the spot.

Why Does an AI Booth Cost More Than a Standard Booth?

A standard open-air photo booth in Ireland starts at €680. An AI booth starts at €900. So you are looking at roughly a €220 premium — here is exactly what that pays for.

AI processing on every single image. A standard booth captures a photo and prints it. An AI booth captures the photo and then runs it through a generative model to restyle it — that is real GPU compute per shot, multiplied across hundreds of guests over the night. It is the single biggest reason the price sits higher.

The software we engineer ourselves. BoothLedger handles the capture, the AI styling, the instant sharing and the live gallery as one system. Building and maintaining that pipeline — rather than renting a generic app — is part of what you are paying for, and it is why the AI output is consistent instead of hit-and-miss.

Higher-spec capture. Good AI output starts with a good source image. We shoot on Canon DSLR and mirrorless bodies with proper lighting, because a clean, well-lit capture is what lets the AI produce a portrait worth printing rather than a muddy mess.

More attendant guidance. Guests need a moment to choose a style and understand what the booth does. That is more hands-on attendant time per guest than a booth where people simply step in and smile.

A bigger creative setup and dye-sub printing. The backdrop, lighting and on-screen experience are a larger build, and every print is a true dye-sublimation print — smudge-proof and instant — not an inkjet sheet.

Factors That Affect Your Final AI Photo Booth Quote

The €900 starting price covers a typical 3-hour weekend booking within easy reach of our Dublin or Athlone hubs. Here is what can move the final number up or down:

Duration. Most events run on 3 hours, which lines up neatly with the post-meal to second-drinks window at a wedding and the peak hour at a corporate event. Extra hours are available at a reduced per-hour rate of roughly €100–€120, cheaper than the base rate because the setup is already done.

Day of the week. Saturdays in June, July, August and December are our busiest dates and price accordingly. Friday nights, Sundays and midweek bookings usually come with better availability and sharper pricing.

Travel distance. With kits based in Dublin and the Midlands (Athlone), we cover most of the country with no extra charge. For very remote venues — far west Mayo, west Cork or Donegal — a small travel supplement applies, and it is always shown up front in the quote, never sprung on you afterwards.

Branding and custom AI styles. Standard overlays with names, dates and hashtags are included. Bespoke AI styles trained to a theme, branded scenes, on-brand overlays and guest data capture sit at the higher end — this is the territory of AI photo booths for corporate events, where the styling is doing real marketing work.

Bundling. Adding a 360 booth, an audio guestbook or a print-based open-air booth alongside the AI booth reduces the per-item cost noticeably. For a lot of weddings, two booths together costs less than one and a half at separate-supplier rates.

Is an AI Photo Booth Worth the Cost?

For the right event, easily — but the honest test is whether the kind of content an AI booth makes is what your guests actually want. We would rather steer you to the right booth than oversell you the flashy one.

You will get the best value if:

  • You have a younger, social-first guest list who will love a styled portrait that is genuinely unlike anything they have posted before
  • You are running a brand event and want shareable, on-theme imagery that does double duty as marketing
  • You want a talking point — the “how did it do that?” reaction is half the fun
  • You like the idea of both a printed keepsake and an instantly shareable digital file from the same booth

A classic photo booth is probably better value if:

  • Your crowd skews older and simply wants a clear, flattering printed photo to take home
  • You want a no-fuss, step-in-and-smile experience with the shortest possible queue
  • Budget is the deciding factor and the AI styling is a nice-to-have rather than the point

How to Get the Best Value on AI Photo Booth Hire

Book early. Our 10–15% early-booking discount applies to AI hires booked more than 6 months out. On a peak Saturday that is the single biggest lever you have on the price.

Bundle a second booth. An AI booth plus an open-air booth, or an AI booth plus a 360, comes in well below the cost of booking each separately — and it gives the older guests a straightforward print while the younger crowd plays with the AI styles.

Move off Saturday if you can. A Friday or Sunday in peak season can save 10–15% on its own. For corporate clients, a midweek activation is better value again.

Pick a venue close to a hub. If your date and venue are still flexible, choosing somewhere within an hour of Dublin or Athlone keeps you in the standard pricing tier with no travel supplement.

Get a written, itemised quote — never a verbal one. We send a full quote with every line included, every limit stated and no asterisks. Ask any supplier to do the same, so you can compare like with like.
