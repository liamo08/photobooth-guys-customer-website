Best Wedding Venues in Westmeath (2026)

If you are getting married in Westmeath, you are spoiled for choice: from lakeshore restaurants on Lough Ree to grand hotels by Lough Ennell and a genuine castle near Castlepollard, the county packs an unusual amount of variety into a small area. Our shortlist of the best wedding venues in Westmeath for 2026 covers eight standout options — what each one is, where it is, and the kind of day it suits — so you can narrow things down quickly.

We have a particular soft spot for Westmeath because it is home turf. Our second hub is in Athlone, right on the county’s western edge, which makes us the local Midlands photo booth company. We have run booths at more than 2,500 events across Ireland, a good share of them within a short drive of here, so we know these rooms, these car parks and these loading doors well. That local knowledge means setup is smooth and, for most Westmeath weddings, delivery is free.

The county sits at the heart of Ireland’s Lakelands, which is part of its appeal for couples. It is genuinely central — guests travelling from Dublin, Galway, the north-west or the south can all reach Mullingar or Athlone without an epic journey — and the lake settings give you scenery that photographs beautifully without anyone having to leave the venue.

The best wedding venues in Westmeath

We have grouped these loosely by setting — lakeside hotels and restaurants first, then the town-centre and parkland hotels around Mullingar, then the historic country houses and castle for couples after something with real character. Each is a working wedding venue we would happily bring a booth to.

Wineport Lodge, Glasson (Athlone)

Wineport Lodge is a lakeshore restaurant-with-rooms on the edge of Lough Ree at Glasson, just outside Athlone. It leans towards intimate, design-led waterside weddings rather than huge numbers, with the lake itself doing a lot of the heavy lifting for photos. If you want somewhere relaxed, contemporary and right on the water, it is hard to beat.

Hodson Bay Hotel, Athlone

Hodson Bay is a large lakeside hotel on Lough Ree, a short spin from Athlone town, and one of the most popular spots in the Midlands for bigger weddings. The scale means it comfortably handles large guest lists and out-of-town family staying over, while still keeping that lake-view setting. It is a reliable choice if your day is on the larger side.

Annebrook House Hotel, Mullingar

Annebrook House Hotel sits right in the centre of Mullingar, built around a restored Victorian house with mature gardens and a dedicated wedding pavilion. The combination is handy: you get period charm and outdoor space for photos, plus a purpose-built room for the meal and dancing. Being in the town also makes it easy for guests on foot or by taxi.

Mullingar Park Hotel, Mullingar

The Mullingar Park Hotel is a modern hotel on the edge of town that is geared towards larger weddings. Its function spaces are built for scale, so if you are planning a big day with a long guest list and want everything under one roof, it is well set up for it. The contemporary style suits couples who would rather not fuss over a heritage building.

Bloomfield House Hotel, Mullingar

Bloomfield House Hotel overlooks Lough Ennell just south of Mullingar, set in its own parkland grounds. The lakeside parkland gives you a peaceful, green backdrop for photos and a bit of room to breathe away from the town. It is a solid middle-ground option — scenic, but still a full-service hotel that can host the whole day.

Belvedere House & Gardens, Mullingar

Belvedere is a historic house and gardens perched above Lough Ennell, a few minutes from Mullingar. It is best known as a scenic backdrop — the gardens, follies and lake views are genuinely lovely for photographs — so couples often pair it with a nearby reception venue or use its event spaces for a more distinctive celebration. Worth a look if heritage and gardens are high on your list.

Crookedwood House, Crookedwood

Crookedwood House is a characterful country house restaurant near Lough Derravaragh, in the village of Crookedwood north of Mullingar. It suits intimate weddings where the food and the setting matter more than scale — think a smaller, warmer day with personality. If you want somewhere that feels like a proper country retreat rather than a function room, this is the one.

Tullynally Castle, Castlepollard

Tullynally Castle, near Castlepollard in the north of the county, is a historic castle with extensive gardens — a heritage setting that lends itself to marquee and estate-style weddings. It is the venue to consider if you have always pictured saying your vows somewhere with history and grounds, and you are open to building your day around an estate rather than a hotel. It is distinctive in a way few venues can match.

Choosing your Westmeath venue

Match the venue to your numbers first. The quickest way to shorten this list is to be honest about guest count. Hodson Bay and the Mullingar Park Hotel are built for large weddings; Wineport Lodge and Crookedwood House shine with smaller, more intimate groups. Picking a venue that fits your numbers naturally avoids a room that feels half-empty or uncomfortably packed.

Decide how much the setting matters. If lake views and photogenic grounds are non-negotiable, the Lough Ree and Lough Ennell venues — Wineport Lodge, Hodson Bay, Bloomfield House and Belvedere — should top your list. If you would rather have everything self-contained and central, a town-centre hotel like Annebrook keeps logistics simple for guests.

Think about the whole guest experience. Consider parking, on-site rooms, how far guests travel and whether there is a comfortable space for an evening reception. A few practical questions worth asking any venue:

  • How many can the room seat for the meal, and how many for the evening?
  • Are there enough on-site or nearby rooms for guests staying over?
  • Is there a dedicated space for evening entertainment — a band, DJ and a photo booth?
  • What is the access like for suppliers loading in and out?
  • Are there outdoor areas for drinks reception and photos if the weather plays ball?

It is also worth mapping your venue choice against your wider plan.
