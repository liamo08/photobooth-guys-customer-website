Top Wedding Photo Booth Ideas for 2026 — Straight From Our Fleet

We have run photo booths at over a thousand Irish weddings. The weddings where the booth was the talk of the night all had one thing in common: the couple chose the right booth, configured it well, and paired it with one or two small extras that multiplied the impact. Here are the ideas that actually work — every one of them built from services we deliver ourselves.

1. Match the Booth Type to the Venue

Our 360 booth looks incredible at a modern hotel reception, but in a period castle ballroom a vintage tripod booth or enclosed photo booth will feel right at home. For stately venues with high ceilings and chandeliers, the selfie mirror is our most-booked option — it reads as a full-length mirror when idle, then comes to life when a guest taps it.

2. A Selfie Mirror for Classic Wedding Elegance

Our selfie mirror is the single most-booked booth for Irish weddings. Full-length interactive mirror, on-screen animations, unlimited instant prints, dedicated attendant. Guests tap the glass, strike a pose, and collect a souvenir strip in under thirty seconds.

3. A 360 Booth for the Dance Floor Crowd

A 360 booth captures slow-motion video as a camera arm spins around your guests. Clips go straight to phones via QR code and end up on Instagram and TikTok within minutes — which means your wedding hashtag actually trends. Line it up with a song the band is about to play and the crossover is electric.

4. A Magazine Booth for the Ultimate Wow Factor

Our magazine photo booth turns every guest into a cover star on a custom magazine layout designed around your names, date and theme. It is our premium product and, for weddings that want one unforgettable talking point, nothing else comes close.

5. A Glam Booth for a Fashion-Shoot Feel

Our glam photo booth uses professional studio lighting and a Kardashian-style black-and-white finish. It is a favourite for hen nights and stylish receptions where guests want to feel like they have stepped into a magazine shoot rather than a reception.

6. Custom Photo Strip Overlays With Your Names and Date

Every booth we deliver includes a custom overlay — your names, wedding date, venue, and any visual theme you want. It is the single biggest reason guests stick the photo on the fridge rather than lose it on the drive home. It is standard with every package, never an add-on.

7. An Audio Guestbook to Go With the Photos

Our audio phone guestbook is a retro handset that records voice messages from your guests. Placed beside the booth, it captures the sound of your wedding alongside the photos. Couples regularly tell us it is the item they replay most on their first anniversary.

8. A Guinness Pint Printer for the Irish Wedding Touch

Our Guinness pint printer prints custom images straight onto the foam of a fresh pint — your names, a photo, or your wedding crest. It is unmistakably Irish, gets a reaction every time, and pairs beautifully with a photo booth stationed near the bar.

9. An AI Photo Booth for Something Nobody Expects

Our AI photo booth transforms guest portraits into themed artwork in real time — renaissance oil paintings, comic book heroes, vintage film stills. It is the booth most guests have never seen before, and the one they will talk about for years.

10. A Roaming Photographer for the Moments Booths Miss

Our roaming photographer captures candid moments out on the dance floor, at the bar, and around the tables — printing and delivering photos on the night. It is the perfect complement to a static booth: guests come to the booth, and the photographer comes to them.

11. Bundle a Booth Plus an Audio Guestbook

The two most-requested extras at Irish weddings are a booth and an audio guestbook. We offer a bundle discount when you book them together, and the combined keepsake — photos plus voice memories from every guest — is hard to beat as a wedding memento.

12. Themed Props Tailored to Your Wedding

Every booth comes with a curated prop box matched to your crowd — oversized glasses, hats, chalkboard signs, feather boas. Tell us in advance if your wedding has a specific theme, GAA rivalry, or running joke, and we will tailor the prop box accordingly.

13. Position the Booth in the Drinks Zone

The single biggest mistake we see is burying the booth in a back room. Put it in direct line of sight of the bar. Guests walking to and from drinks cannot miss it, queues form naturally, and the booth stays busy from the first hour to the last. Your dedicated attendant will position it correctly on the night.

14. Time It for the Post-Speeches Dead Hour

Book a three-hour slot starting right after speeches — typically 8pm to 11pm. That window covers the lull when the meal is done and the band is setting up, then carries into the first hour of dancing. It is when guests most need something to do, and when a booth earns its keep.

15. Bundle Two Booths for Bigger Weddings

For weddings over 150 guests a single booth creates queues. Pairing a selfie mirror with a 360 booth gives you two very different experiences running side by side — classic keepsake prints on one, sharable slow-mo video on the other. We offer multi-booth discounts when both are booked together.

The Principle Behind Every Idea

Every item above does one of three things: it makes the booth more visible, it makes the photos more personal, or it gives your guests a reason to come back for a second or third turn. Those are the three levers every wedding booth decision should pull.
