Why We Built BoothLedger

Running Photobooth Guys across Ireland, we tried every photo booth software on the market. None of them truly understood what operators need at live events — reliability under pressure, instant sharing, and the kind of creative AI effects that make guests stop in their tracks.

So we built our own. BoothLedger is the result of years of real-world event experience distilled into software that actually works when it matters most.

SMS, email, QR code, and AirDrop sharing built right in. Guests get their photos within seconds — while the excitement is still fresh. Custom branding on every touchpoint.

No Green Screen Needed
