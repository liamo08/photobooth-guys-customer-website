Best Wedding Venues in Tipperary (2026)

Tipperary is one of those counties that quietly has it all when it comes to weddings – rolling farmland, mountain backdrops, historic houses and a couple of genuinely world-class hotels. We have set up our photo booths in venues right across the county, so we wanted to put together an honest guide to the places couples keep coming back to. From the five-star Cashel Palace Hotel in the shadow of the Rock of Cashel to the relaxed country charm of Cloughjordan House, there is something here for almost every kind of wedding.

What we love about Tipperary is the variety. You can have an exclusive-use manor house where the whole place is yours for the weekend, a grand hotel ballroom that handles a big guest list with ease, or a small private castle for an intimate gathering. Venues like Kilshane House and Aherlow House Hotel show off two very different sides of the same county.

Below are the venues we rate most highly, grouped by the kind of day they suit best. We have stuck to places that are real, currently operating, and easy enough to verify – and we have been honest about what each one is actually like rather than just listing names. If you are still deciding, hopefully this gives you a useful starting point.

Why Tipperary works so well for weddings

Tipperary sits right in the middle of the country, which makes it surprisingly easy for guests travelling from Dublin, Cork, Limerick and Galway alike. That central location is a big part of why so many couples with family scattered across Ireland end up choosing a Tipperary venue – nobody has an impossible drive, and the motorway network keeps things straightforward.

The other thing the county has going for it is scenery and history in equal measure. You have the Glen of Aherlow and the Galtee Mountains on one side, the Rock of Cashel and a string of restored historic houses on the other, and plenty of working countryside in between. It photographs beautifully in almost any weather, which is exactly what you want from an Irish wedding day.

The best wedding venues in Tipperary

Cashel Palace Hotel (Cashel)

In the town of Cashel, this five-star hotel is a restored Palladian manor dating from 1732 and a Relais & Châteaux property. It sits directly below the Rock of Cashel, with several acres of gardens and a Michelin-starred restaurant on site, making it one of the most luxurious wedding venues in the county.

Kilshane House (Bansha)

Just outside Bansha, Kilshane House is a country mansion built in 1822 and set on a large estate of private parkland. It is run as an exclusive-use venue, with a curvilinear conservatory and an orangerie that couples often use for ceremonies and receptions.

Hotel Minella (Clonmel)

On the banks of the River Suir in Clonmel, Hotel Minella is a long-established family-run hotel set between the river and the Comeragh Mountains. It is a popular choice for larger weddings, with extensive grounds and a good range of bedrooms and suites on site for guests.

Aherlow House Hotel (Glen of Aherlow)

Tucked into the Glen of Aherlow overlooking the Galtee Mountains, Aherlow House Hotel is one of the most scenic venues in the county. Alongside the hotel rooms there are self-catering lodges in the grounds, which makes it a practical choice for couples who want guests to stay on site.

Cloughjordan House (Cloughjordan)

Near the village of Cloughjordan in north Tipperary, Cloughjordan House is a historic manor house set in its own countryside grounds. It runs as a relaxed, home-from-home exclusive venue with its own gardens, lawns and a strong focus on home-grown and locally sourced food.

Bansha Castle (Bansha)

Also in Bansha, near the Glen of Aherlow and the Galtee Mountains, Bansha Castle is a privately owned period house dating from the 18th century. With its turf fires and period features, it suits smaller, more intimate wedding celebrations and family gatherings.

Ashley Park House (Nenagh)

Just outside Nenagh, Ashley Park House sits on the edge of Lough Ourna among beech woodland and formal gardens. It is a tranquil, characterful country house venue for couples who want something quieter and more private away from the larger hotels.

Racket Hall Country House Hotel (Roscrea)

In Roscrea in north Tipperary, Racket Hall is a country house hotel with walled gardens and grounds. Its function suite is a long-standing local choice for weddings, and the on-site accommodation keeps things convenient for guests staying over.

Choosing the right Tipperary venue

The best venue for you really comes down to the kind of day you are picturing. A grand five-star hotel, an exclusive-use manor and a small private castle all create very different atmospheres, so it is worth being honest early on about whether you want something polished and formal or relaxed and intimate.

Once you have a shortlist, visit in person and ask plenty of practical questions before you commit – and give yourself enough runway to plan properly. Our wedding planning timeline for Ireland walks through when to lock in each supplier so nothing gets left to the last minute. A few things worth weighing up:

  • Guest numbers: some venues shine with 250 guests, others are built for 60 – match the room to your list.
  • Travel: think about how far your key guests have to drive and whether the location is easy to reach.
  • Accommodation: check how many guests can stay on site and what the nearby options are.
  • Style: decide whether you want a hotel ballroom, a country house or an exclusive-use property.
  • Date: popular Tipperary venues book out well in advance, so flexibility on dates helps a lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best wedding venues in Tipperary?

Some of the most popular wedding venues in Tipperary include Cashel Palace Hotel in Cashel, Kilshane House near Bansha, Hotel Minella in Clonmel, Aherlow House Hotel in the Glen of Aherlow and Cloughjordan House in north Tipperary. The right one for you depends on your guest numbers, budget and the style of day you want.

How far in advance should I book a Tipperary wedding venue?

For the most popular Tipperary venues we would suggest booking 12 to 18 months ahead, especially for peak summer Saturdays. Exclusive-use properties and the larger hotels tend to fill their best dates earliest, so the sooner you can confirm your date the better.

Are there exclusive-use wedding venues in Tipperary?

Yes. Venues like Kilshane House and Cloughjordan House are run on an exclusive-use basis, meaning the property is yours for the celebration rather than shared with other events. This suits couples who want a private, relaxed weekend with their guests rather than a busy hotel setting.
