AI Photo Booths for Corporate Events & Brand Activations

An AI photo booth turns your guests into branded content creators — it takes a normal photo and reimagines it in your brand’s world, then hands the guest a finished, shareable image they actually want to post. For a corporate event or brand activation, that means on-brand media generated at the event itself, by the very people you want talking about you. We’ve delivered booths for brands including Meta, LinkedIn and the Guaranteed Irish Business Awards, and the AI booth is the one marketing teams ask us about most.

A standard AI booth starts from €900, with corporate and branded activations sitting higher depending on the custom AI styling, branding and data capture you need. Below we’ll walk through exactly why these booths earn their keep at corporate events, the activation use cases they suit, how the branding and lead capture actually work, and how to measure whether it was worth it.

Why AI booths work so hard at corporate events

A regular photo booth gives people a nice keepsake. An AI booth gives your marketing team something it can rarely buy: genuine earned media, made on the spot. When a guest sees themselves rendered as a piece of street art in your campaign colours, or dropped into a generated scene that matches your launch, they post it — not because you asked them to, but because the image is good. That post carries your branding, your hashtag and your overlay out to their network, which is exactly the audience a B2B brand wants to reach.

There are four jobs an AI booth quietly does all night:

  • Earned media at scale. Every guest who shares becomes a small, credible endorsement. The output is novel enough that share rates are high — people post AI images they’d never bother posting a flat snapshot of.
  • A footfall magnet on a stand. At an exhibition or conference, an AI booth pulls a crowd. A queue at your stand is a queue away from a competitor’s, and it gives your team a natural reason to start a conversation.
  • A memorable experiential moment. The reveal — watching the AI transform your face in a few seconds — is a genuine talking point. It’s the bit people describe to colleagues the next day.
  • Reusable content for the team. Every image lands in a cloud gallery you can download, so the marketing team walks away with a library of on-brand, consented user-generated content to reuse across channels.

Brand activation use cases

The AI booth flexes to suit very different corporate briefs. These are the formats we’re booked for most often:

  • Product launches. Generate AI backgrounds that place guests inside the campaign world — the new product, the hero colour, the launch tagline — so every photo reinforces the message you’re launching.
  • Conferences & summits. A booth in the networking area gives delegates a reason to linger, and the branded output keeps your event hashtag trending through the day.
  • Trade-show stands. This is where AI booths shine for lead generation. Our trade-show photo booth hire setup is built to draw footfall and capture details at the same time.
  • Experiential & pop-ups. For a short, high-impact activation, the AI reveal is the experience — no other prop generates the same dwell time and shares in a small footprint.
  • Festivals. High-volume, high-energy environments where a fast, novel, shareable output keeps a queue happy and your brand visible across thousands of posts.
  • Staff parties & awards nights. The kind of polished evening event we run regularly — think the Guaranteed Irish Business Awards or a black-tie night at a venue like the Conrad Dublin — where a branded AI booth becomes the social centrepiece without feeling like an ad.

Custom AI styles = your brand world

This is the part that separates a corporate AI booth from a novelty one. The AI doesn’t have to use generic filters — we tune the styling to your brand so the output looks like it came from your campaign team, not a stock effect.

  • Style transfer in your brand aesthetic. We theme the AI render to your colours and visual language — the graffiti-and-street-art look, an editorial illustration style, a painterly campaign treatment — so every image is unmistakably yours.
  • AI-generated backgrounds. Drop guests into a scene that matches your campaign world: the new product environment, a destination, an abstract brand space. The guest stays the hero; the background does the brand work.
  • Branded start screens. The booth’s welcome screen carries your logo and campaign creative, so the experience is branded from the first tap.
  • Overlays, frames and hashtags. Every finished image carries your logo, frame, campaign hashtag and any handle you want pushed — baked in, so it travels with the post.

Data & lead capture

For a B2B activation, the photos are only half the value — the other half is knowing who came to your stand. The AI booth can capture leads cleanly, but it has to be done properly, with consent at the centre.

  • Opt-in delivery. Guests choose to receive their image by email, SMS or QR code. That delivery step is the natural, non-pushy moment to capture a contact detail — they’re giving it to get their photo, not because a salesperson cornered them.
  • GDPR-aware consent. Capture is opt-in, with clear wording on what the detail is used for and a separate tick if you want permission to follow up for marketing. We keep the data flow tight and the consent explicit, because under GDPR the photo and the contact detail are both personal data.
  • Exportable for your CRM. Consented contacts and the gallery export in a clean format your team can drop straight into the CRM or marketing platform after the event.

We’ll always frame the consent step with you in advance rather than guess — the wording is your call as data controller, and we build the booth flow to match it. Our booths run on BoothLedger, the photo-booth software we built and maintain in-house, which is what lets us tailor that capture-and-delivery flow precisely instead of relying on a locked-down third-party app.

Measuring ROI

A booth that can’t be measured is hard to re-book. The good news is an AI activation produces clear numbers. After the event we can report on:

  • Shares and posts — how many images guests pushed out to their own networks.
  • Social impressions and reach — the onward audience those branded posts and your hashtag reached.
  • Dwell time at the stand — how long guests engaged, a strong proxy for stand performance at a trade show.
  • Leads captured — the count of consented, exportable contacts handed to your team.
  • Volume of images generated — total interactions, the headline number for total engagement.

Put those next to the hire cost and the maths usually speaks for itself, especially when you weigh the consented content library against what an agency photo shoot for the same volume of usable assets would cost.

AI booth vs other corporate booths

The AI booth isn’t always the right answer, and we’d rather steer you to the booth that fits the brief. Here’s how we think about it.

Choose the AI booth when the goal is shareable, novel content and standout at a crowded event — a launch, a flagship stand, an experiential pop-up where you want people stopping and posting. The transformation is the hook, and the output is unique enough to travel further than a standard photo.

Choose a branded open-air booth when you want high throughput, a clean, predictable, heavily logoed print and a lower price point — great for awards nights and large dinners where you want to photograph hundreds of guests fast. A 360 booth wins when you want a premium video moment for social rather than a still image. We lay out the full range, including these options side by side, on our corporate photo booth hire page and in our guide to corporate event photo booths, so you can match the booth to the outcome you’re actually after.

Logistics

An AI activation is straightforward to host once you know what it needs. A few practicals:

  • Space. Allow roughly a 2m x 2m footprint for the booth and a little room around it for the queue and the photo area. We’ll confirm exact dimensions against your floor plan.
  • Power. A standard mains socket within reach is all we need.
  • An experienced attendant. Every booking includes a trained attendant for the full hire — the same team that builds the software runs your event, so if anything needs tweaking on the day it’s handled on the spot rather than over a helpline.
  • Setup time. Setup and pack-down are free on top of your hire; we arrive in good time to have everything tested and on-brand before doors.
  • Lead time for custom styling. Bespoke AI styles, generated backgrounds and branded screens take design and testing time. Book early — for anything fully custom we like several weeks’ lead, and for large campaigns more. The earlier we start, the more refined the look.
