How Much Does a Selfie Mirror Cost in Ireland? 2026 Pricing Guide

A selfie mirror — also called a digital mirror — costs from €730 to hire in Ireland in 2026, for a standard 3-hour package with everything included. That single inclusive figure covers delivery, setup, a trained attendant for the full hire, unlimited prints, a custom overlay and the online gallery, so there is no long list of extras stacked on top.

That said, €730 is a starting point rather than the whole story. The final number on your quote moves with the date, the venue, the hire length and whether you want a custom backdrop or branding. This guide breaks down exactly what a selfie mirror costs, why it sits a little above a basic photo booth, and how to get the most out of your budget.

Selfie Mirror Price at a Glance

Here is what selfie mirror hire costs in Ireland in 2026, in plain numbers:

  • Standard selfie mirror (3 hours): from €730, fully inclusive
  • Extra hour: typically €100–€120 per additional hour, charged at a reduced rate
  • Selfie mirror + 360 booth bundle: from around €1,300 — cheaper than hiring the two separately
  • Selfie mirror + audio guestbook bundle: from around €1,050, pairing prints with a recorded message keepsake
  • Custom backdrop or branding add-on: sits a little higher, depending on whether you want a flower wall, a sequin wall or a fully branded overlay and wrap

What Is Included in Selfie Mirror Hire?

Every selfie mirror we deliver across Ireland comes with the same fully inclusive package. The €730 is not a stripped-back base price with hidden charges waiting on the day — it covers the lot:

  • Delivery, setup and pack-down — we arrive 60–90 minutes before guests do, position and calibrate the mirror, run the touchscreen and lighting test, then pack down quietly at the end. Setup and pack-down time is free on top of your 3 hours
  • Trained booth attendant — with the mirror for the full hire, welcoming guests, guiding the animations and keeping the prints flowing
  • Unlimited high-quality prints — no cap on how many your guests take home on the night
  • Custom photo overlay — names, wedding date, hashtag or company branding designed to match your event
  • Online digital gallery — every shot from the night in one shareable link the morning after
  • Instant sharing — guests can have their photo by SMS or email within seconds, alongside the print
  • Props and backdrop — a quality prop set and a standard backdrop are included as standard
  • Touchscreen animations and effects — the signature swiping graphics, on-screen signing and voice prompts that make the mirror feel theatrical

Like all our booths, the selfie mirror runs on BoothLedger — the photo-booth software we built and maintain in-house. The same team that writes the software runs your event, with a Canon mirrorless camera doing the capture, so it does not fall over on the night.

Why Does a Selfie Mirror Cost a Bit More Than a Basic Booth?

A basic open air photo booth in Ireland starts at €680. A selfie mirror starts at €730. So you are looking at roughly a €50 premium — what does that actually buy you?

The full-length interactive mirror hardware. A selfie mirror is a large, framed, full-height mirror with a camera and display built behind the glass. It is a more expensive, more delicate piece of kit per event than a tripod-mounted camera and a backdrop, and it needs more care in transport and setup.

The touchscreen and animation tech. Guests touch the glass to start, watch animated graphics sweep across the mirror, sign their photo with a finger, and follow voice prompts. That interactive layer is what people remember — and it is genuinely more software and more hardware than a point-and-shoot booth.

The mirrorless Canon capture. The mirror shoots through a quality Canon mirrorless camera rather than a webcam, so the prints and digital files hold up properly. That image quality is part of what you are paying the small premium for.

The theatrical experience. A selfie mirror is a centrepiece. It draws a crowd, the animations entertain the queue, and the full-length format means group shots and full-outfit photos work in a way a tabletop booth cannot match. For weddings especially, that presence is the point.

Factors That Affect Your Final Selfie Mirror Quote

The €730 starting price covers a typical 3-hour weekend booking within about 90 minutes of our Dublin or Athlone hubs. Here is what can move the final number up or down:

Duration. Most events run on the standard 3 hours, which lines up neatly with the post-meal-to-dancing window at a wedding. Extra hours are available at a reduced rate of roughly €100–€120 each — cheaper per hour than the base package because the setup is already paid for.

Day of the week and season. Saturdays in June, July, August and December are our busiest, most in-demand dates. Friday nights, Sundays and midweek bookings usually come with better availability and sharper pricing.

Travel distance. We base kits in Dublin and the Midlands (Athlone, Westmeath), which covers most of the country at no extra charge. For very remote venues — far west Mayo, west Cork or Donegal — a small travel supplement applies, and it is always shown in the quote up front, never sprung on you later.

Backdrop and branding. A standard backdrop is included. If you want a flower wall, a sequin wall, a bespoke printed backdrop or fully branded on-screen graphics and a frame wrap for a corporate event, that sits a little higher than the base price. It is worth getting a custom backdrop priced alongside the mirror rather than as an afterthought.

Bundling. Adding a 360 booth, an audio guestbook or a second print booth alongside the mirror reduces the per-item cost noticeably. For a lot of weddings, two booths together costs less than one and a half would at separate-supplier rates — and you cover more of the guest list at once.

Is a Selfie Mirror Worth the Cost?

For most weddings and big parties, yes — but the honest answer depends on what you want from the night and how tight your budget is.

The selfie mirror is the most theatrical of the print-based booths. It is a genuine centrepiece, it photographs full-length so group shots and outfits land properly, and the touchscreen animations keep the queue entertained rather than bored. At weddings, milestone birthdays and glam parties, it pulls people in all night. For a deeper look at the experience and how it stacks up against a conventional booth, our selfie mirror vs photo booth for weddings comparison is worth a read.

You will get the best value from a selfie mirror if:

  • You want a statement centrepiece that guests gather around, not just a corner booth
  • Full-length group photos and full-outfit shots matter to you — weddings, debs, glam parties
  • You like the idea of guests signing their own prints on the glass
  • You want both a physical print and an instant digital share

A plain open air booth may be better value if:

  • Your budget is genuinely tight and that €50 matters — the open air booth gives you the same unlimited prints and the same inclusive package for less
  • Your venue space is limited and a large full-length mirror is awkward to fit
  • You care more about the prints than the interactive, theatrical presentation

How to Get the Best Value on Selfie Mirror Hire

Book early. Our 10–15% early-booking discount applies to mirrors booked more than 6 months out. For peak Saturday dates, that is the single biggest lever you have on the price.

Move off Saturday if you can. A Friday or Sunday booking in peak season can save 10–15% on its own, and midweek is cheaper again. If your date has any flexibility, it pays.

Bundle a second product. A mirror plus a 360 booth, or a mirror plus an audio guestbook, comes in well below the cost of booking each separately — and it spreads the entertainment across more of your guests.

Pick a venue close to a hub. If your venue choice is still open, somewhere within an hour of Dublin or Athlone keeps you firmly in our standard pricing tier with no travel supplement.

Get a written, itemised quote. Always. We send a full quote with every inclusion listed, every limit stated and the travel supplement (if any) shown up front — ask any supplier to do the same so you are comparing like with like.
