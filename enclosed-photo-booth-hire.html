Perfect for Weddings

Enclosed Booth at Weddings

The enclosed photo booth is the classic wedding photo booth. The curtain creates a private, intimate space where guests let loose and have fun away from the crowd. Couples, families, and friend groups pile in for candid moments you will not get anywhere else.

The classic photo strip format — three or four shots in a column — is the original photo booth keepsake. Guests take one strip home and leave a duplicate in the wedding guest book with a personal message. It is the most popular wedding photo booth format in Ireland.

  • Private curtained booth for intimate, candid photos
  • Classic photo strips for guest book & keepsakes
  • Custom prints with couple's names & wedding date
  • Fits into any wedding venue reception room

Fully Brandable

Custom Branded Enclosed Booth

The entire exterior shell of the enclosed booth can be fully wrapped with your brand. Custom vinyl wraps transform the booth into a branded centrepiece that doubles as event decor and a marketing tool.

We have created custom branded enclosed booths for brands like Orchard Thieves, wrapping the full exterior with campaign artwork, logos, and brand colours. The booth becomes part of the activation — not just entertainment, but a branded experience guests interact with and photograph from the outside too.

  • Full exterior vinyl wrap with your brand artwork
  • Custom branded curtain & interior
  • Branded photo strips with logos & campaign messaging
  • Perfect for brand activations & corporate events

Branded Photo Strips

Custom Photo Strip Prints

The enclosed booth prints classic photo strips — the iconic column of three or four photos that everyone loves. Each strip is fully customisable with your branding, event name, and custom design.

For corporate clients like LinkedIn, we brand every strip with the company logo and event theme. For weddings, we add the couple's names and date. The photo strip is the ultimate keepsake — guests keep them in wallets, stick them on fridges, and pin them to notice boards for years.
