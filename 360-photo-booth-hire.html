Space, Power & Setup

What Your Venue Needs To Host a 360 Booth

We've set up at castles, country estates, converted warehouses, marquees and GAA clubs. Here is what the booth needs from the space — we handle everything else.

Floor Space

Minimum 3m x 3m of clear, level floor. Carpet, timber, concrete and tiled surfaces all work. We bring a matting for uneven ground.

Ceiling Height

Minimum 2.5m (8ft) so the camera arm rotates freely. High-ceilinged venues like Tankardstown House and The Marker are no issue.

Power

A single standard 13A socket within 5m of the platform. We bring extension leads, surge protectors and all cabling.

Weight Limit

The platform is rated to 200kg / 31 stone. Comfortable for groups of 2–4 adults. Non-slip surface and low step-up.
