About Us

About Photobooth Guys — Ireland's Engineer-Led Photo Booth Company

Photobooth Guys isn't your average photo booth hire company. We're a team of engineers who fell in love with events — and then built the technology to run them better than anyone else. From our base in Ireland we cover the whole country, from Dublin city-centre product launches to coastal weddings on the Wild Atlantic Way, and we've become the go-to photo booth partner for blue-chip brands and event agencies across Ireland.

Our Story

Engineers First, Event People Always

Most photo booth companies buy an off-the-shelf booth and a stock software licence. We took a different road. As engineers, we wanted total control over reliability, image quality and the guest experience — so we designed our own booths and wrote our own software from the ground up. That obsession with getting the technology right is exactly why our booths simply work, every single time, even at remote venues with no Wi-Fi and no mains power.

Over 1,000 events later — weddings, corporate galas, brand activations, debs and parties the length and breadth of Ireland — that engineering-led approach has earned us a perfect 5.0 rating on Google & WeddingsOnline and the trust of some of the biggest names in Irish business.

Our Technology

We Built BoothLedger — Used Worldwide

Our in-house platform, BoothLedger, started life as the software running our own booths. It worked so well that photo booth companies around the world now license it to run theirs. Booking management, on-site capture, AI effects, instant printing and digital sharing — it's all powered by software we wrote and maintain ourselves.

For you, that means a partner who controls the entire stack: when you hire us, there's no third-party software to blame, no licence limits, and new features land faster than anyone else in the market. Read more about BoothLedger.
